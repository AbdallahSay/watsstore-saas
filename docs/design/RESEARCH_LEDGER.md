Scope: desktop concept images, inspected as a contact sheet. Nine distinct designs
were visually accessible. Six additional image downloads and sixteen live brand
pages were blocked or returned access-denied/challenge pages. Those are not counted
as inspected designs. The requested 15–20-reference target was not reached.

## Direction

Editorial: inset split composition, warm paper, restrained captions, category sidebar.
Streetwear: cover campaign composition, bold display scale, orange action, dense product rack.
Lifestyle: arch crop, earthy colors, offset three-column rhythm and open category navigation.
All use actual merchant imagery, factual catalog counts and functional commerce controls.
Arabic uses the existing Cairo font; English editorial headings use the available system
serif stack. Mobile stacks content and restores regular grids for usable comparison.
Shared checkout uses readable neutral surfaces, minimum touch targets, trapped dialog
focus, Escape dismissal and reduced-motion styles. No review or trust claims are invented.

Research sources are visual inspiration only; no claims about live checkout usability
or mobile behavior are inferred from these screenshots.
