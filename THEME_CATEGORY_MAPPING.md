## 2. Dynamic Feature Specialization

Each theme activates targeted interactive components suited for its shoppers:

### 1. Fresh Market (`fresh-market`)
- **Direct in-card quantity steppers**: Shoppers can tap `+` or `-` directly on the catalog card to adjust quantity in 1 tap without opening a product modal.
- **Speed grocery cart tally**: Immediate subtotal update in header bag.

### 2. Street Pulse (`street-pulse`)
- **Top drop marquee**: Animated scrolling banner with exclusive drop announcements and urgent FOMO text.
- **Brutalist Sold-Out Stamp**: High-contrast red/black sold-out overlays.

### 3. Tech Grid (`tech-grid`)
- **Hardware Spec Badge**: `⚡ ضمان معتمد 100% · كفالة الوكيل`.
- **Cyber grid matrix**: Sharp 6px borders with electric cyan glow on hover.

### 4. Golden Atelier (`golden-atelier`)
- **Karat purity stamp**: `✨ عيار أصلي موثق ومعتمد`.
- **Fine gold border**: Delicate gold filigree border with velvet shadow depth.

### 5. Pure Beauty (`pure-beauty`)
- **Formula pill tag**: `🌿 تركيبة نباتية لطيفة · خالٍ من العطور`.
- **Soft organic radius**: 20px card radius with subtle rose-tinted box shadow.

### 6. Crafted Story (`crafted-story`)
- **Artisan story card**: Prominent blockquote section with authentic maker statement.
- **Stitched craft tag**: `🧵 صُنع يدوياً بحب وعناية`.

### 7. Little Joy (`little-joy`)
- **Kid-safe certification**: `🧸 آمن للأطفال 100% وخالٍ من المواد الضارة`.
- **Ultra-rounded buttons**: Bouncy 24px radius and cheerful color contrasts.

### 8. Living Space (`living-space`)
- **Architectural arches**: Hero and catalog products feature 48% arch borders.
- **Material chip**: `🪵 خامات طبيعية مصممة للمنزل`.

### 9. Active Motion (`active-motion`)
- **Pro performance chip**: `⚡ أداء رياضي فائق · مقاوم للتعرق`.
- **Dynamic angled buttons**: High-energy athletic CTAs.

### 10. Editorial Luxe (`editorial-luxe`)
- **Index numbering**: Sequential two-digit numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`).
- **Classic Roman Serif**: High-fashion typography.
